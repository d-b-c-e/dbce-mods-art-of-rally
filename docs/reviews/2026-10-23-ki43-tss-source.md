# KI-43 current-source TSS bind review

Read-only review at source `5cca4d3a42791700a7fedcffbb2ab00a8eaad62d` against `docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md` KI-43. The owner-installed 0.4.2-rc.28 comes from `eb2316dc5bc951557c9bf1f9c2abbe0c6b2118ab`; the only change in the relevant `WheelInput.cs` between that source and this HEAD concerns clutch and leaves the handbrake Bind/Calibrate path unchanged. No game, USB device, UI, install, heavy build or broad test was run.

## Source path

- `SettingsPanel.Controls` places **Handbrake (axis)** and **Handbrake (button)** on separate rows. Each row calls `WheelInput.IsBound` and `Describe` for its own channel; a bound axis can therefore coexist with a button row showing `not assigned`. The axis row also shows its own live `Device input` percentage. `Use assigned controls` Off explicitly warns that the separate handbrake is Off while game-bound wheel and pedals may still work. Bind/Calibrate/Clear are disabled while the stage is driving.
- `WheelInput.BeginCalibration` calls `BeginAssign`. Routine Bind retains open responsive DirectInput readers; it refreshes only if no readers are open, a reader failed/missing, or a nonempty attached-device catalog changed. An empty enumeration preserves responsive handles. A previously listed reader that failed to open makes `NeedsRefresh` true, so paused Bind retries it. On each usable reader it captures axis/button/hat baselines. During assignment `UpdateCore` samples those handles without discovery; a failed initial read obtains a new baseline on recovery rather than counting the recovery as movement. Shared-profile ownership refuses an Art editor for the handbrake axis; that is an explicit mode gate.
- Fresh analog Bind needs one unambiguous axis move of at least `AssignThreshold` from its baseline. The candidate stays provisional. For **Calibrate**, `BeginCalibration(channel, true)` resolves the *saved* device by GUID (or unique legacy name), clones the binding, and keeps its saved axis index; another moving axis cannot replace it. Missing saved device/baseline cancels with a specific status and retains the binding.
- `StepCalibration` follows the candidate's actual reader/axis, records the travel, and marks release when raw input returns within 2000 counts of rest. `CanSaveCalibration` requires release and sufficient travel (both sides for steering; rest-to-far for a pedal/handbrake). The panel disables Save until then. `SaveCalibration -> Bind -> SettingsCommit.TrySave` stores the axis binding and enables assigned controls only on a successful settings save; failed save restores the prior in-memory setting and leaves the candidate for retry. `Binding.Parse/ToString` retain GUID, calibration and inversion; `LoadBindings` accepts the analog handbrake separately from the button channel. `Resolve` follows GUID across device reorder, and the read value reaches `WheelInputPatch.Override` as an analog maximum with the stock and optional button handbrake.
- Existing `tests/WheelInput/CalibrationTests.cs` has focused simulated-device cases for responsive-reader retention, empty enumeration, full travel/release/Save, saved-axis Calibrate, failure/retry, inversion, independent axis/button and disconnect; `tests/WheelInput/Program.cs` covers failed-open retry and late USB device. These are fixture coverage, not a T300/TSS result. No test was rerun for this document-only review.

## Decision and remaining acceptance

No concrete current-source KI-43 defect is established by these paths, and the historical 0.2.6 reader-closing/axis-switching defects are addressed in current source. Preserve the **external T300/TSS fresh-bind acceptance gate**: capture a support file while failure is present, before reinstalling; record paused state, assigned-controls/shared-profile status, reader list and first raw TSS axis read, Bind status and provisional candidate, full travel and release, Save result/Settings.xml, and which of the two handbrake rows says `not assigned`. Existing saved-axis preview and MOZA persistence do not prove a fresh TSS bind or the game's in-race label. No patch or feature claim follows from this source review.
