# Art of Rally standalone controls source trace — October 23

Scope: main `5cca4d3`, read-only source/fixtures and player instructions. No package, installed game, UI, device, force, display, lease or heavy build was touched. The owner-accepted installation identified by `AGENTS.md` is private 0.4.2-rc.28; this source review does not retest it.

## Three saved routes

1. **Game controls.** Art's stock controls screen binds Rewired actions with its `ControlsRemapper`; the game's `Rewired.Data.UserDataStore_PlayerPrefs` saves maps and calibration under `HKCU\Software\Funselektor Labs\art of rally` (`docs/CONTROLS.md`, `docs/FINDINGS.md`). Those Rewired values reach `AxisCarController.GetInput`. They are separate from the mod's files.
2. **Mod F6 assigned controls.** `SettingsPanel` offers **Use assigned controls** and Bind/Calibrate for physical DirectInput axes and buttons. `WheelInput.LoadBindings/Bind` reads and saves channel assignments through `Settings.xml`; `SettingsCommit.TrySave` retains the previous setting on failed binding saves. `WheelInputPatch.Override` runs after `AxisCarController.GetInput`, replacing only bound steering, throttle, brake, clutch and handbrake values; unbound values stay with the game's Rewired read. `GameButtonInput` supplies eligible named Rewired button and menu queries. A player can use this route without Wheelkit or the standalone helper.
3. **Private shared profile.** The separate Controls Setup download's `Set Up Controls.bat` runs `Setup-Controls.ps1`, which checks its full manifest and pinned helper, an installed shared-input candidate and a closed game, then launches DBCE Mod Setup locked to Art. Wheelkit Core's `ArtShimControls.TryPlan` checks candidate metadata, the mod/shim/native payload pins, the settings and profile hashes, and supported actions. Its targets write `Mods/ArtOfSimRally/dbce-controls.ini` plus `SharedInput=true` in `Mods/ArtOfSimRally/Settings.xml` (and a compatible transmission choice when applicable). `tools/Test-StandaloneSetupPackage.ps1` has extracted-helper preview/apply, preservation and changed-shim refusal fixtures. This is the standalone helper's source and fixture write authority, not proof of an owner Apply.

At next game start, `Main` loads `Settings.xml`; `SharedInputStartup.Prepare` freezes the profile bytes and inspects the pinned shim before any reader qualification. In the menus, `SharedInputSession` waits for readers and force reconnect to settle, isolates Rewired's physical wheel input, verifies neutral and attaches the source to `WheelInput`. `WheelInput` publishes one shared snapshot per frame. `ShimInput.TryOverride` composes owned axes into the same `AxisCarController.GetInput` postfix; unowned axes may keep Art's saved binding from that shared copy when it does not duplicate a mapped physical control. `GameButtonInput` maps supported shared actions into the game's Rewired queries; Art-only buttons retain guarded local bindings. A failed attached session does not resume a second physical wheel reader in the same process.

The main-menu F6 **Shared profile** editor loads the frozen startup bytes. `SharedBindingCaptureHost.Save` writes its draft to `dbce-controls.ini`; that save is for the next launch, not hot replacement of the attached reader. It is separate from `Settings.xml` and the game's PlayerPrefs. The companion does not calibrate axes; its `README.txt` directs partial-range adjustment to device software. Art's own F6 calibration applies to Art assignments, not shared bindings.
